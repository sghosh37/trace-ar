How to use it

From choosing a picture to tracing it, and getting back to it after the headset sleeps.

Quick start

  1. Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser. To install, use the browser's menu → Install app.
  2. Choose an image on the app page: it opens straight away, floating in front of you. Hold the trigger to carry it, Lay flat to put it on a table, then Lock.
  3. Or press Start tracing to select a surface first and project the image onto it (steps below).
  4. Trace. If the headset ends the session, come back here and press Resume tracing.

Surface selection and tracing in detail

  1. Select the surface (Lasso). Point at the surface and tap the trigger to drop dots — lines join them automatically. Or hold the trigger and drag to draw freehand. Tap near the first (yellow) dot, or press Done, to close the shape. The app then measures the surface inside the shape by itself (keep your hands out of the way for a second).
    Detect shows the objects your headset already knows from Space Setup (tables, desks, walls…). Point at one and pull the trigger — its outline loads as dots you can drag to fine-tune, then press Done.
    Other tools: Sweep (hold the trigger and paint over the surface) and Quick flat (tap once on flat paper).
  2. Pick the image. Press Image on the panel to open the gallery. It shows pictures from the images folder next to this app on your PC (Downloads\TraceAR\images), plus any picked on the app page.
  3. Map the image. The image is projected onto the scanned shape, so it bends over curves. Hold the trigger on the surface to slide it around; use the panel to resize, turn and set opacity.
  4. Move the panel: point at one of its four corner handles, hold the trigger (or pinch) and drag. It stays where you leave it. Hide and show it again to bring it back in front of you.
  5. Hide the panel with Hide (or click the left thumbstick). A small Show panel button floats above your left controller to bring it back. Steady: Low / Medium / High on the scan panel sets how much the pointer is smoothed.
  6. Sway your head a little: if the image slides over the paper, press Raise / Lower (1 mm steps) until it stays put — then it sits exactly on the paper.
  7. Gap between your line and the image? Tilt your head: if the gap changes, use Raise / Lower; if it stays the same, nudge with Up / Down / Left / Right. These six buttons keep working while locked, in exact 1 mm steps.
  8. Lock (panel) and trace. A locked image is frozen in place (to unlock, hold the button for a moment). Opacity still works while locked.
  9. Resume: every time you lock, the tracing is saved together with an anchor the headset remembers in your room. If the headset ends the session (for example after a long sleep), the start page shows Resume tracing: press it and the image goes back onto the same place on your paper, locked. If the headset can't find it, the image appears in front of you, unlocked, to carry back and lock again.
  10. Blink: after locking, the image blinks for 5 s as a preview. Set Blink / sec on the panel (0 = off, up to 50 per second) to keep it blinking — handy for checking your lines against the paper.
    Caution: fast flashing (roughly 3–30 per second) can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Above about 36 per second the headset switches to its fastest display mode; very fast blinking looks like a dimmer, shimmering image rather than separate blinks.
