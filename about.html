About Trace AR

Project a picture onto paper, a wall or any 3D surface in Meta Quest passthrough, lock it in place, and trace it by hand.

Trace AR is a personal, open-source alternative to tracing apps like TraceArtist, made for the Meta Quest 3 / 3S. It runs in the Quest Browser as a WebXR page and can be installed from the browser so it appears in your App Library.

  • Any surface: select paper on a table, a wall, or a curved object (lasso, sweep, Space Setup objects or one-tap flat). The image is projected onto the real shape, so it bends over curves.
  • Precise on paper: calibrate the desk by resting a controller on it. The controller's tracking is far more precise than depth sensing, so the image sits right on the paper instead of a centimetre above or below. Move the image with your hand and twist your wrist to turn it.
  • Steady when locked: a locked image is frozen to the real world; only 1 mm Raise / Lower and nudges still move it.
  • Resume: every lock is saved with a room anchor. If the headset ends the session after a long sleep, one press puts the image back on the same place on your paper.
  • Private and offline: your images stay inside the app on the headset. Nothing is uploaded. After the first visit it works without internet.

What you need

  • A Meta Quest 3 or 3S (depth sensing is used to hit real surfaces) and the Meta Quest Browser.
  • Space Setup done on the headset, if you want the Detect tool to show your tables and walls.
  • Internet for the first visit only. After that it works offline.
